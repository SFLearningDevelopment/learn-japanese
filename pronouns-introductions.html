💡 Did You Know?

Japanese has multiple levels of formality! わたし (Watashi) is the standard "I", while あなた (Anata) for "you" is often avoided in favor of using the person's name with さん (San). Children's names often use ちゃん (Chan) or くん (Kun), and せんせい (Sensei) is used for teachers and doctors.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Japanese Family Structure

Learn how to refer to your own family and someone else's family in Japanese

💡 Did You Know?

Japanese has two different sets of words for family members depending on whose family you are talking about. When referring to your own family, you use humble/plain words (e.g. ちち for father). When referring to someone else's family, you use honorific words with さん (e.g. おとうさん). Using the wrong set can sound impolite, so it's important to learn both!

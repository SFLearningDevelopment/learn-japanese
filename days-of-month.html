📅 Days of the Month in Japanese

Learn how to say dates in Japanese with audio pronunciation

💡 Did You Know?

Japanese dates use special counters that are highly irregular from 1st to 10th! The first day is ついたち (Tsuitachi), not いちにち. Days 1–10, 14, 20, and 24 have unique readings derived from native Japanese numbers. Other dates follow a more regular pattern using Sino-Japanese numbers + にち (nichi). This makes learning dates one of the trickiest parts of Japanese!

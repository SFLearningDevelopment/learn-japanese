💡 Did You Know?

In Japanese, まい (mai) is the counter used for flat, thin objects such as paper sheets, tickets, plates, shirts, and leaves. It follows the standard Chinese-derived number system: いち、に、さん and so on, combined with まい. This makes it one of the most regular and easy counters to learn in Japanese!

💡 Did You Know?

Some numbers have two readings in Japanese! For example, 4 can be よん (Yon) or し (Shi), and 7 can be なな (Nana) or しち (Shichi). Large numbers use まん (Man) for 10,000 and おく (Oku) for 100,000,000 — quite different from the Western system!

📦 Counting Things in Japanese

Learn how to count objects in Japanese with audio pronunciation

💡 Did You Know?

Japanese uses native counting words called 和語数詞 (wago-suushi) for counting general objects from 1 to 10. These end in つ (tsu) for most numbers, except 10 (とお) which is irregular. Beyond 10, Japanese switches to Chinese-derived numbers with specific counters. This set of words is one of the most fundamental in everyday Japanese!

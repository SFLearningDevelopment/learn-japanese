🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Counting Persons in Japanese

Learn how to count people in Japanese with audio pronunciation

💡 Did You Know?

Japanese has special counters for counting people using にん (nin). However, 1 person (ひとり) and 2 persons (ふたり) are irregular — they use native Japanese words instead of the usual pattern. From 3 onwards, the pattern becomes regular: number + にん (nin).

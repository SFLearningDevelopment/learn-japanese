💡 Did You Know?

In Japanese, じ (ji) means "o'clock" and is added after the number. Note that 4 o'clock is よじ (Yo-ji) — NOT よんじ, and 7 o'clock is しちじ (Shichi-ji) — NOT ななじ. 9 o'clock is くじ (Ku-ji) — NOT きゅうじ. These three are the most common mistakes learners make!

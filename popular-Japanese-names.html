💡 Did You Know?

In Japan, the surname (family name) comes first, followed by the given name — the opposite of Western convention. So "Sato Taro" means the person's family name is Sato (さとう) and given name is Taro (たろう). It is also polite to add さん (San) after someone's name as a sign of respect.

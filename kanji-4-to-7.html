💡 Kunyomi vs Onyomi

Every Kanji has two types of readings:
訓読み Kunyomi (Japanese reading) — the native Japanese pronunciation, used when the Kanji appears alone or with hiragana.
音読み Onyomi (Chinese reading) — the Chinese-derived pronunciation, often used in compound words with other Kanji.
